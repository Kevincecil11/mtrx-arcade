House rules

Fair play,
no tricks

01

Honest difficulty

Levels are tested against each other: each one beats the level below it. Every coin flip uses your device's secure random generator, so no two games repeat.

02

Learn without losing your place

Rules, strategy, techniques and drills live in the menu. Opening it pauses the clock and hides the board until you come back.

03

Practice or Challenge

Practice gives you the coach and free undos. Challenge takes the coach away, adds time penalties, and keeps a separate best-times board.
